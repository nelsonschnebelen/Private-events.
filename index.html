What bookers told us

We studied why people don't book. Fix these four things and you win.

Event planners and party hosts shop three to five venues at once. The patterns in what makes them choose — and ghost — are remarkably consistent.

The fastest clear answer wins

Bookers send multiple inquiries at once. The venue that replies first with complete information — not "let's hop on a call" — takes the deposit. Most venues take days. Beat them with hours.

Fix → the 2-hour proposal
Hidden fees kill trust

A $95/guest quote that becomes $128 after service charge, tax, and fees is the #1 reason contracts stall. Regulators are now mandating all-in pricing — get ahead of it and say the real number first.

Fix → all-in instant quote
Nobody wants to repeat themselves

Being bounced from host stand to manager to chef — re-explaining the party each time — is the most-cited frustration in event booking. One named captain, first call to final toast.

Fix → one point of contact
"Inquire for pricing" is a dead end

Stale photos, vague capacity, and pricing behind a form make bookers bounce. Publish real menus, real minimums, and live-feeling availability — self-service until they're ready to talk.

Fix → everything on the page

The standard

The Booker's Bill of Rights — publish it and mean it.

Every one of these answers a real frustration bookers named. Print them on your events page. Almost none of your competitors will match them.

A complete proposal within 2 hours, in business hours

Not an acknowledgment. Not "let's find time to chat." The menu, the room, the all-in price, the date held for 72 hours.

The all-in price, first

Service charge, tax, and fees in every number we show you. The quote you see is the contract you sign.

One captain, start to finish

You'll have a name and a direct line. You will never re-explain your event to a second person.

Dietary needs handled by default

Vegan, gluten-free, allergies, kosher-style — flagged once on the form, confirmed on the menu card, no side conversations.

Plain-language deposit & weather-proof cancellation

25% holds the date. Full transfer to a new date with 14 days' notice. No fine print you need a lawyer for.

The experience

Script four moments. The reviews write themselves.

A great private event isn't a long dinner — it's a short story. Engineer these beats into every package and hosts rebook before dessert.

01 · Arrival

The Welcome

Coats taken, host greeted by name, a glass in hand within ninety seconds of the door.

02 · Dinner

The Reveal

One theatrical course — the tableside pour, the carving, the flame — that everyone photographs.

03 · Peak

The Toast

Lights nudge down, mic appears, the captain cues the host. The moment the night is remembered by.

04 · Exit

The Send-off

A wrapped treat and a handwritten card at the door — with next year's booking link on the back.
