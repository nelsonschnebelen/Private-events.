Fourth · The Experience

Engineer four moments. The reviews write themselves.

A great private event isn't a long dinner — it's a short story. Script these beats into every package and hosts will rebook before dessert.

01 · Arrival

The Welcome

Coats taken, host greeted by name, a glass in hand within ninety seconds of the door.

02 · Dinner

The Reveal

One theatrical course — the tableside pour, the carving, the flame — that everyone photographs.

03 · Peak

The Toast

Lights nudge down, mic appears, the captain cues the host. The moment the night is remembered by.

04 · Exit

The Send-off

A wrapped treat and a handwritten card at the door — with the booking link for next year on the back.
